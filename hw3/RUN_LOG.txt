Notable issues hit and fixed during development (see AI_USE.md for full narrative)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. AttentionLM's causal mask was originally sized to the full 51-token sequence but training
   feeds 50-token shifted input, causing a RuntimeError (tensor size mismatch). Fixed by slicing
   the mask to the actual input length inside forward().
2. An early run of the Tree-of-Thoughts branch-generation call hung indefinitely (5+ hours, near
   0% CPU) with no client-side timeout and no cap on generation length, most likely a runaway
   repetition loop server-side. Fixed by adding `num_predict=400` (hard cap on generated tokens)
   and `client_kwargs={"timeout": 120}` (hard request timeout) to every ChatOllama instance, then
   re-ran successfully end-to-end in the log-confirmed run above.
